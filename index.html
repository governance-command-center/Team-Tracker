Scope
All brands for work covering everything you handle; Brand specific requires Brand, Platform and Region.
Brand
The client or store this work is for — e.g. Acme Co. Separate several with commas. Hidden when scope is All brands.
Platform
Where it's happening: Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Amazon.
Region
Market or territory — PH, SG, APAC, NA.
Status
Active = ongoing this week. In progress = actively being worked right now. Done = finished, no follow-up needed.
Owner
The single person accountable for this item — who to ask about it. Different from Members, which can list everyone helping.
Due date
Optional deadline. Items past their date show as overdue; items due within 7 days show as due soon.
Priority
High = blocks someone or has a hard deadline. Medium = normal. Low = nice to have.
Blocked
Tick when the work can't move forward. Blocked items are counted in the banner at the top of the board so the team can unblock them first.
Category
Required. The bucket of work. Pick Others only if nothing fits, then describe the work in Remarks. Admins manage this list under Data.
Focus this week
Required. One short line on what you're actually doing — "Fix rejected listings for Acme", not "listings".
Members
Who is doing the work. Separate names with commas. Only you (or an admin) can change this after saving.
Remarks
Progress since last week, supporting details, and anything stopping you or who you're waiting on — all in one box. Start a line with "-" for a bullet.
Attachment
Optional screenshot as evidence — a ticket, dashboard, or error message.
